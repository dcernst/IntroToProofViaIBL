Theorem 9.46.

Let \(\{A_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\) be a collection of sets. Define \(B_1\coloneqq A_1\) and for each natural number \(n>1\text{,}\) define
\begin{equation*} B_n\coloneqq A_n\setminus \bigcup_{i=1}^{n-1}A_i\text{.} \end{equation*}
🔗
Then we we have the following:
  1. The collection \(\{B_n\}_{n=1}^{\infty}\) is pairwise disjoint.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. \(\displaystyle \bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}A_n =\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}B_n\text{.}\)
